About This Project

A sustainability tool built for real deployment

SolarSoiled was developed for Better Behavior Foundation, a California nonprofit focused on sustainability. The goal is practical: give communities and operators a way to know which solar arrays are losing energy to soiling, without expensive sensors or manual surveys. Everything runs on public data that already exists.

Stage 1: Detection mAP50: 0.630

Array detection on Santa Cruz NAIP imagery. Target is 0.70. Active retraining in progress with improved labels.

Stage 2: Risk Model 0.728 CV AUC

Soiling risk trained on 1,000 NREL panel-years across 15 states. Spatial cross-validation gate cleared. Holdout validation in progress.

The Problem

Soiling Costs the Solar Industry $3-5 Billion Annually

3-5% Average annual energy loss from soiling worldwide
25%+ Loss in arid regions without proactive cleaning
$40B Projected solar O&M spend by 2030, much of it reactive

Most operators rely on fixed cleaning schedules or costly soiling sensors. Both leave energy on the table: either cleaning too early or discovering losses too late. There is no scalable way to see what is actually on your panels.

The Approach

See Every Array. Know Which Ones Need Attention First.

SolarSoiled pulls public aerial imagery for any area of interest, runs a computer-vision model to detect every solar array, and scores each one for soiling risk using weather patterns, land cover, air quality, and site context. No flights, no hardware, no sensors required.

  • Array detection from public NAIP aerial imagery
  • Risk scoring from weather, land cover, air quality, and location
  • Works anywhere in the continental US
  • Outputs as GeoJSON, CSV, or interactive dashboard
How It Works

From Public Imagery to Cleaning Priorities

01

Define an Area

Provide a county, city, or bounding box. The system pulls the latest public aerial coverage automatically.

02

Detect and Score

A computer-vision model finds every solar array. A risk model scores each one using weather, location, and environmental data.

03

Map the Risk

A georeferenced risk map highlights which arrays are losing the most energy and should be cleaned first.

04

Act on It

Array locations and risk scores export as GeoJSON or CSV, or feed directly into cleaning schedules and homeowner outreach.

Santa Cruz Pilot

From Satellite to Doorstep: the Santa Cruz Pilot

We ran the full pipeline on Santa Cruz County: detected and scored 334 rooftop solar arrays, identified the 50 highest-risk installations, and built a complete outreach loop from detection to homeowner contact. This is deployed and running, not a concept.

📬

Personalized Mailers

Each postcard shows the homeowner's specific array area, soiling risk score, estimated performance loss, and recommended cleaning window. Generated automatically from the pipeline output.

📱

QR Code to Dashboard

One scan lands the homeowner on their array, highlighted on the map. They can adjust their last-cleaned date, recalculate risk, and see the energy and money recovered by cleaning.

📊

Three Soiling Models

The dashboard lets users compare our ML model (0.728 CV AUC), the SOMOSclean physics model, and the Kimber 2007 model side by side, so every score is transparent and cross-checked.

💰

Energy and Money Calculator

Enter system size, electricity rate, and peak sun hours. The dashboard shows annual energy lost, annual dollar loss, and estimated recovery from a cleaning.

What It Produces

Everything Needed to Act

The pipeline outputs are designed to connect directly to cleaning operations and community outreach.

Array Detections

Georeferenced GeoJSON of every detected array with model version and confidence on every polygon.

Per-Array Risk Scores

A 0-1 soiling-risk score for each array from three independent models: ML, physics-based, and empirical.

Interactive Dashboard

Live dashboard with map, energy calculator, model comparison, and personalized cleaning recommendations.

Cleaning Recommendations

Risk scores paired with 7-day weather forecasts and last-cleaned date to identify the best cleaning windows.

Homeowner Mailers

Print-ready postcards per array with soiling facts and a personalized QR code. Print-and-mail at low cost per card.

Outreach Targeting

Ranked list combining detection confidence and risk score. Parcel join for owner names and mailing addresses.
