The Problem

Soiling Costs the Solar Industry $3–5 Billion Annually

3–5% Average annual energy loss from soiling worldwide
25%+ Loss in arid regions without proactive cleaning
$40B Projected O&M spend by 2030 — much of it reactive

Most operators rely on fixed cleaning schedules or costly soiling sensors. Both leave money on the table: you either clean too early or discover losses too late. There's no scalable way to see what's actually on your panels.

The Solution

See Every Array. Know Which Ones Need Attention First.

SolarSoiled pulls public NAIP 60cm aerial imagery for your AOI, runs a YOLOv11 segmentation model to detect every solar array, and scores each one for soiling risk using weather, land cover, and site context. No flights, no hardware — just an AOI and a refresh cadence.

  • Array-level detection via YOLOv11 polygon segmentation
  • Built on public NAIP aerial imagery — nothing to capture or upload
  • Spatial risk scoring from weather, land cover, and site context
  • Built for beta pilots before full production rollout
How It Works

From AOI to Cleaning Order in 4 Steps

01

Pick an AOI

Define a county, site, or bounding box. We pull the latest public NAIP 60cm aerial coverage automatically.

02

Detect & Score

Our YOLOv11 model finds every array, then a risk model scores each one from weather and site context.

03

Generate Risk Map

A georeferenced risk map highlights the highest-priority arrays for your cleaning crew.

04

Export & Act

Pull array polygons and per-array risk scores as GeoJSON or CSV, or fetch them via the beta API.

Product Outputs

What You Get Today — and What's Next

Beta partners get the first three deliverables today. The cleaning recommendation engine is the next thing we’re shipping, and your feedback shapes how it works.

Array Detections

Georeferenced GeoJSON of every detected array in your AOI, with model version and confidence on every polygon.

Per-Array Risk Scores

A 0–1 soiling-risk score for each array, derived from weather, land cover, elevation, and site context — refreshed daily.

Structured Data & API

GeoJSON, CSV, and a beta REST API for /detect and /risk. Every response carries model version and quality metadata.

Cleaning Recommendations

Rule-based /recommend endpoint ships today — pairs risk scores with 7-day weather forecasts and your last-cleaned date to flag the optimal cleaning window. ML-based ranking follows once we have partner feedback to train against.
